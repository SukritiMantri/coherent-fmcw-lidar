1Parameters & geometry no lens

Optical (λ = 1550 nm, w₀ = 12.5 mm source = 25 mm collimated beam, ap_radius = 20 mm), chirp (B = 1 GHz, T = 10 µs), operating range (R = [1, 100, 200] m, R_nominal = 100 m).

Source represents the output of a beam expander telescope. Full source chain (laser diode → fiber → amplifier → expander) deferred to a future notebook.

▼

2Build source basis & LSQ (one-time)

9×9 = 81 Gaussian beamlets at w_basis = 12.5 mm, spacing 6.25 mm. Tikhonov-regularized LSQ projects the source field onto the basis.

A = GᴴG + λI ; b = GᴴE_src ; c_src = A⁻¹b // matched basis → residual ≈ 1e-8

▼

3Design LO for nominal R (one-time) ★

The LO's q-parameter is evolved to the round-trip plane of the nominal range R = 100 m. This same LO is used at every R below — modeling production LO that is built once.

LO_q_at_design = (2·R_nominal) + source_basis[0]['q_anchor'] = 200 - 316.7j → w = 14.8 mm, R = 700 m at receive plane

Mismatch at off-nominal R is the realistic feature — academic codes hide it by rebuilding the LO per measurement.

▼
4a · Set up the chirp slices
Pick N_slices to satisfy Nyquist on the beat frequency at this R.
N_slices ≥ 2·f_beat·T = 4·B·R / c For R = 1 m: ~200 | R = 100 m: ~2000 | R = 200 m: ~3000
4b · Compute instantaneous k(t_i)
For each slice, the laser is at a slightly different optical frequency.
f(t_i) = f₀ + (B/T)·t_i ; k_i = 2π·f(t_i) / c
4c · Evaluate signal field at z = 2R (per slice)
Full GBD basis sum at receiver plane using slice-specific k_i. Carries the propagation phase exp(i·kz·2R) that rotates with k.
E_signal_i = basis_sum(basis, c_src, X_g, Y_g, k_i, z = 2·R)
4d · Evaluate LO field at z = 2R (per slice)
LO state has z_anchor = 2·R so dz = 0 → no propagation phase. q is the FIXED design q.
E_LO_i = evaluate_beam_state(lo_state, X_g, Y_g, k_i, z = 2·R)
4e · Coherent overlap (inside aperture)
Restricted to the 20 mm aperture mask. Complex-valued.
overlap_i = Σ E_signal_i[mask] · E_LO_i*[mask] · dx²
4f · R extraction — Method A (phase slope)
After all slices: unwrap arg(overlap) vs t, fit a line. Slope = 2π·f_beat. Sub-mm precision, no FFT bin limit.
phases = np.unwrap(np.angle(overlaps)) slope, _ = np.polyfit(t_slices, phases, 1) R_phase = (slope / 2π) · c·T / (2·B)
4g · R extraction — Method B (photocurrent FFT)
Build real photocurrent, FFT, find peak. Matches what real hardware measures. Bin-limited to ~15 cm.
I_t = 2·Re(overlap) peak = argmax(|FFT(I_t)|[positive]) R_fft = freqs_pos[peak] · c·T / (2·B)
4h · Heterodyne efficiency η (normalized)
Standard formula, exact numerical integration over the aperture. Captures both LO mode-mismatch and aperture-clipping effects.
η = |Σ E_sig · E_LO* · dx²|² / (Σ|E_sig|²·dx² · Σ|E_LO|²·dx²)

Design notes

★ Time-resolved chirp. The FMCW beat is not constructed from a precomputed tau = 2R/c. It emerges from how the propagation phase exp(i·k(t)·2R) rotates as k changes during the chirp. R is recovered from the rotation rate.

★ Collimated beam. No focusing lens. Beam stays approximately TX-aperture-sized across the operating range (since Rayleigh range zR ≈ 316 m exceeds R = 200 m). Matches production geometry; replaces the toy 1 mm + 1 m-lens used in the other notebooks.

★ Single LO design. Built once for nominal R = 100 m. Reused at all R values. Realistic LO mismatch at off-nominal R produces the η dip — the actual tolerance behavior of production systems.

★ What's deferred. Full laser-source chain (EELD, fiber, amplifier, beam expander), atmospheric turbulence, diffuse-target speckle, detector noise, scanner. Each is its own future extension.
