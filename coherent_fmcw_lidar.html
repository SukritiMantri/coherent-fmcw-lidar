1Parameters & grids

Optical (λ=1550 nm, w₀=1 mm, f_lens=1 m, R=100 m, v=10 m/s, z_receive=2R=200 m), FMCW (B=1 GHz, T=10 µs, fs=200 MHz), basis 9×9 with 1.5 mm spacing, scan grid 5×5 in ±1 mrad.

LSQ grid: ±20 mm × 401² | Viz grid: ±2000 mm × 513² (wide enough to hold all 81 beamlets after scanner+lens redirect)

▼

2Build source basis (81 beamlets at z=0)

9×9 grid of w=1 mm Gaussian beamlets at z=0, on the 1.5 mm-spacing source grid.

make_source_state(w, k, x0, y0, z0=0) → q_anchor = -i·zR

▼

3Decompose Gaussian source → c_source

Tikhonov-regularized least squares (λ_reg=1e-8) projects E_src = exp(-(x²+y²)/w₀²) onto the 81-beamlet basis on the LSQ grid.

A = GᴴG + λI ; b = GᴴE_src ; c_source = A⁻¹b

Sanity 1: ‖G·c − E_src‖ / ‖E_src‖ at the basis-decomposition floor.

▼
4a · Scanner
Apply uniform tilt to every beamlet — pure kx/ky bump.
kx ← kx + k·sin(θx) ; ky ← ky + k·sin(θy)
4b · Thin lens (at z=0)
Per-beamlet q-parameter update plus a position-dependent tilt (the lens phase gradient).
1/q' = 1/q − 1/f ; kx ← kx − k·x_at_lens/f
4c · Propagate to z = 2R = 200 m
Round-trip via image method (planar mirror = forward propagation by 2R). Sum all 81 beamlets on the viz grid → E_signal(x,y).
4d · Predict spot position
Geometric-optics target landing point.
x_spot = 2R·tan(θx) ; y_spot = 2R·tan(θy)
4e · Build mode-matched LO ★
LO must match q_anchor, position, AND tilt. Position-only matching collapses off-axis overlap by 3–6 orders of magnitude.
lo: q=q_signal, (x,y)=(x_spot,y_spot), kx=k·sin(θx), ky=k·sin(θy)
4f · Coherent overlap integral
Spatial mode-overlap between signal and LO at the receive plane.
overlap = ∫∫ E_signal · E_LO* dx dy ≈ Σ E_s·E_L* · dx²
4g · FMCW signal synthesis
Triangular chirp produces two beat tones from the optical round-trip delay + Doppler shift.
τ = 2R/c ; f_D = 2v·f₀/c f_up = (B/T)·τ − f_D f_down = (B/T)·τ + f_D I_up(t) = 2|overlap|·cos(2π f_up t) I_down(t) = 2|overlap|·cos(2π f_down t)
4h · FFT → measured beats
Peak-pick on positive-frequency FFT bins gives f_up_m, f_down_m.
4i · Recover (R, v)
R_meas = (f_up_m + f_down_m) · c·T / (4B) v_meas = (f_down_m − f_up_m) · c / (4·f₀)
4j · Spatial centroid (sanity)
x_c = Σ |E|²·x / Σ|E|² (likewise y_c)
▼

5Aggregate 25 LiDAR points

Each scan direction → tuple (θx, θy, R, v, x_centroid, y_centroid, overlap). Stack arrays for stability statistics.

Design notes

★ Mode-matched LO. Must match all of (q_anchor, x, y, kx, ky). Position-only matching causes off-axis overlap to collapse by 3–6 orders of magnitude — easy bug to miss because the on-axis direction still works.

★ Grid extent. Off-axis beamlets after scanner + lens land far from the origin at z = 2R. Viz grid must be wide enough (±2000 mm here) to capture all 81 beamlets without clipping.

★ Nyquist. Max unambiguous R = (fs/2)·c·T/(2B) = 150 m with these parameters; we sit comfortably at R = 100 m.
